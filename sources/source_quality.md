## Overall assessment of the source base

- **Strongest:** 1848–1990 (rich German institutional + international academic + primary sources).
- **Adequate:** Medieval and early modern (institutional + key revisionist scholarship; fewer primary sources read directly).
- **Weakest / most uncertain:** Antiquity (Roman sources + contested archaeology); quantitative estimates before 1800; "what ordinary people believed" before modern polling.
- **Present-day items** are reliable as of 27 Sept 2026 but will date quickly.
