## B. Topics covered only thinly here (worth deeper research)

- **Austria after 1866** and the großdeutsch idea's afterlife (Anschluss 1938).
- **Regional histories:** Bavaria, Saxony, the Rhineland, Hanseatic cities, Silesia, East Prussia — each with its own trajectory.
- **Jewish history** as its own continuous story (not only persecution): medieval ShUM culture, Haskala, emancipation, Weimar culture, post-1990 revival.
- **Rural and agrarian history** after 1850 — farmers' politics, the Agrarian League, collectivisation in the GDR.
- **Environmental history** — forests (the 18th-century invention of "sustainability" in Saxon forestry), industrial pollution (Ruhr, Bitterfeld), the Green movement.
- **Science and universities** — the German research university, Nobel-era science, Nazi "racial science," the post-war brain drain.
- **Cultural history** — Weimar modernism, Bauhaus, film, post-war literature (Gruppe 47), GDR culture.
- **Gender and sexuality** — beyond the outline here: Paragraph 175's history, the women's movements, East–West family models.
- **Germany and the Global South** — beyond Namibia: East Africa (Maji Maji), China (Qingdao), Pacific; GDR's "solidarity" with the Global South.
- **Minority language communities** — Sorbs, Danes, Frisians, Sinti and Roma.
- **Everyday life in the GDR** — consumption, housing, youth cultures, the church.
