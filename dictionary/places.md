<a id="aachen"></a>

- **Kind:** place · city
- **Short:** City in western Germany near the Belgian and Dutch borders; Charlemagne's favourite residence.
- **Explanation:** Where German kings were crowned as kings (imperial coronation took place in Rome) — a tradition linking them to Charlemagne.

### Agri Decumates
<a id="agri-decumates"></a>

- **Kind:** place · region
- **Dates:** until c. 260 CE (Roman)
- **Short:** Roman-ruled land between the upper Rhine and Danube, in today's south-west Germany.
- **Explanation:** This angle of territory, roughly today's Baden-Württemberg, lay inside the Roman frontier (the Limes). When Rome abandoned the upper Limes around 260 CE, the Alamanni settled there — the origin of the Alemannic south-west.
- **German:** Dekumatland
- **Main chapter:** [`ancient/02_roman_frontier.md`](../ancient/02_roman_frontier.md)
- **Also in:** `02_MASTER_TIMELINE.md`
