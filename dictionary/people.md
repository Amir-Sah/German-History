### Adam Tooze
<a id="adam-tooze"></a>

- **Kind:** person · economist
- **Dates:** b. 1967
- **Also written as:** Tooze
- **Short:** British economic historian of the Nazi war economy who rebutted Götz Aly.
- **Explanation:** In 'The Wages of Destruction' (2006) Tooze showed that about 70% of war costs came from German taxes and borrowing and that consumption was suppressed. He argues the regime was held together by coercion, ideological passion and war, and that Germany mobilised heavily from the start.
- **Main chapter:** [`twentieth_century/07_world_war_two.md`](../twentieth_century/07_world_war_two.md)

### Adolf Eichmann
<a id="adolf-eichmann"></a>

- **Kind:** person · other
- **Dates:** 1906–1962
- **Also written as:** Eichmann
- **Short:** SS officer who organised the deportation of Europe's Jews to the killing sites.
- **Explanation:** Eichmann headed the Gestapo office responsible for deportations, arranging trains and timetables that took Jews from across Europe to ghettos and death camps. He escaped to Argentina, was captured by Israel, tried in Jerusalem and executed in 1962.

### Adolf Hitler
<a id="adolf-hitler"></a>

- **Kind:** person · politician
- **Dates:** 1889–1945
- **Also written as:** Hitler
- **Short:** Leader of the Nazi party; appointed chancellor in 1933, then dictator of Germany until 1945.
- **Explanation:** After a failed coup (1923) he pursued power through elections, but his party never won a free majority; conservatives had him appointed chancellor in January 1933. Within months he built a dictatorship; from 1934 he was 'Führer and Reich Chancellor'. His ideological goals were indispensable to the regime's war and genocide.

<a id="alan-milward"></a>

- **Dates:** 1935–2010
- **Short:** British economic historian behind the older 'Blitzkrieg economy' interpretation.

### Albert Speer
<a id="albert-speer"></a>

- **Kind:** person · politician
- **Dates:** 1905–1981
- **Also written as:** Speer
- **Short:** Hitler's architect and armaments minister 1942–45 who later posed as an apolitical technocrat.
