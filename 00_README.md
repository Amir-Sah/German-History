## What this is

This knowledge base reconstructs the **"operating system" of German society** from the Roman frontier to the Federal Republic of 2026: who held power, how institutions actually worked, how ordinary people lived, why one system turned into the next, and where historians disagree.

It is **not** a list of dates to memorise. Dates are used as anchors only.

## Five warnings before you start

1. **"Germany" is not one continuous thing.** Keep these apart: *German-speaking lands* (a language zone), *German states* (dozens of polities), *the German nation* (an idea, strong only from c.1800), *the German Empire* (1871–1918, a specific state), *modern Germany* (the Federal Republic). See `01_RESEARCH_METHOD.md §5`.
2. **Formal institutions ≠ real power.** Almost every regime here looked different on paper from how it worked.
3. **Official ideology ≠ what people believed.** Propaganda, sermons and party programmes tell you what elites wanted people to believe.
4. **Nothing was inevitable.** Several "roads to 1933" narratives were written backwards from the catastrophe; the files flag where historians now reject them.
5. **Numbers are anchors, not facts to memorise.** Where figures are contested (Thirty Years' War deaths, WWI hunger deaths, denazification statistics) the files say so.

## Scope note

Austria and the Habsburg lands are treated as part of the German story until 1866 (they were central to the Holy Roman Empire and the German Confederation), and as a neighbour afterwards. Swiss, Dutch, Baltic and other German-speaking or formerly German-ruled areas appear where relevant but are not followed in depth.

## Status & limits

- Research conducted with web access to institutional, academic and primary sources in German and English (see `sources/`). Some paywalled monographs were accessed only through reviews and summaries; this is marked in the source files.
- Present-day facts (2025–26 elections, court rulings, statistics) are current to **late September 2026** and will date quickly.
- Video sources are listed as **leads**; they were not treated as evidence for any key claim.
